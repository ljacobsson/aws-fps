# Storylines

## A — One night shift (picked)

- **Device / form:** a moment in someone's day · a flight through one world. The intern clocks in and walks the building room by room, each world entered through the game's own doors; the night ends in the Service Graveyard.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** a fast, neon tour of a living account (Lambda racks, EventBridge cron gauges, CloudWatch walls, a stack room craning up), then the mood drops into a moonlit graveyard of retired AWS services and ends on "F to pay respects".
- **First two seconds:** first-person walk across the lobby toward the glowing AWS logo; "Your AWS account." lands in Anton.
- **Beats:** hook (lobby) → Lambda → EventBridge → CloudWatch "WANTED" gag → CFN door portal → stack crane-up (payoff 1) → fade to black → graveyard walk → tracking shots along the tombstones → EC2-Classic, RESPECT + / MISSION PASSED → title card + `npm start`.
- **Look:** the game itself: night-black data center, AWS orange `#ff9900`, Anton titles with hard 3 px black drop shadows, Rajdhani sub-lines — GTA loading-screen typography on real gameplay.
- **Sound:** the game's server hum under act one, every room change on the game's whoosh, near-silence and wind in the graveyard, the game's mission jingle on MISSION PASSED, one bass impact on the title.

## B — Loading screens (not built)

- GTA loading-screen art: each service is a "character" freeze-frame with a hand-painted comic look and a name card, cut on the beat between bursts of gameplay. Needs a music bed to drive the cuts.

## C — One continuous take (the bold one, not built)

- The camera never cuts: it walks out of every room's door straight into the next, the game's door portals carrying it from lobby to graveyard in one unbroken flight. Bold, but 60 s of one move gets monotonous without music.
